Questions people ask

How much does junk removal cost?

Single items start at $70. Bigger jobs are priced by how much of our 5x10 trailer your junk fills, from $150 for a quarter load to $425 for a full load. Text us photos and we'll send a price range.

Can you come today?

Often, yes. Call or text early in the day and we'll try to fit you in. Most pickups happen within 24 hours.

Do I need to be home?

No. Send us photos, we'll agree on the price by text, and you leave the items where we can reach them. You pay by Zelle when we're done.

Will you take things from inside the house or upstairs?

Yes, at no extra charge. We carry everything out ourselves.

What won't you take?

Hazardous chemicals, wet paint and solvents, asbestos, medical waste, propane tanks and fuel. Everything else, just ask.

How do I pay?

Cash, Zelle or check, after the job is done.
