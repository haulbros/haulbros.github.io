FAQ

Questions? We've got answers.

How fast can you come out?

Most pickups happen within 24 hours, and same-day is often possible. Call or text early in the day for the best chance.

How much will my job cost?

Single items start at $70. Bigger jobs are priced by how much of our 5x10 trailer they fill, from $150 for a quarter load to $350 and up for a full one. Text us photos and we'll send your price.

Do I need to move anything to the curb?

No. We take things from inside, upstairs, the attic or the backyard at no extra charge. If your items are already at the curb, you get $20 off.

Do I have to be home?

No. Send photos, we agree on the price by text, and you leave the items where we can reach them. Pay by Zelle when we're done.

What won't you take?

Hazardous chemicals, wet paint and solvents, asbestos, medical waste, propane tanks and fuel. Everything else, just ask.

How do I pay?

Cash, Zelle or check, once the job is done.

Which areas do you cover?

Fulshear, Katy, Richmond, Rosenberg, Sugar Land, Brookshire, Sealy, Cypress and most places within about 35 miles of Fulshear.
